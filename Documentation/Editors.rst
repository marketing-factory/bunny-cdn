.. _editors:

=============
For editors
=============

Nothing to configure or do differently. Saving, publishing, or deleting
content automatically purges the affected page (and its Bunny cache) on all
sites — no manual cache clearing needed.

If a page still shows old content after editing:

*  Check whether the change is on a workspace that hasn't been published yet.
*  Give it a moment — the purge happens right after saving but Bunny needs a
   short moment to apply it.
*  If it persists, contact your administrator: it may indicate the CDN
   integration is disabled or misconfigured.
